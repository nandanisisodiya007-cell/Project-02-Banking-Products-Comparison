Banking Products Comparison – SBI and HDFC

Project Overview

This project compares three banking products offered by State Bank of India (SBI) and HDFC Bank. It evaluates interest rates, tenure, minimum deposits, maturity amounts, withdrawal rules, flexibility and deposit protection.

The objective is to understand banking products and identify suitable options based on a sample customer's financial goals.

Products Compared

Product| Bank| Interest Rate| Tenure
Savings Account| SBI| 2.50% p.a.| No fixed tenure
Recurring Deposit| SBI| 6.25% p.a.| 12 months
Fixed Deposit| HDFC Bank| 6.25% p.a.| 12 months

Rates are based on the project research and are subject to change.

Project Objectives

- Compare banking products from two banks.
- Understand interest rates and deposit requirements.
- Calculate maturity amounts and interest earned.
- Compare flexibility and early withdrawal conditions.
- Understand deposit insurance and associated risks.
- Recommend a suitable product based on customer requirements.
